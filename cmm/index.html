    Capability Maturity Model

    Team Training#

    Required before entering the second part of the method.

    DBJ CMM is TOGAF CMM tailored for businesses. All roles, but architects. They already know the complex CMM, designed for teams of architects.

    DBJ CMM measures organisational maturity on the scale L0 to L5, mapped one to one onto TOGAF’s M0 to M5. Two levels decide what an organisation can do:

    • L3 (M3) — Defined. The first level at which work can be done at all: the DBJ ADM wheel turns. Clearance to enter the track, not the destination.
    • L5 (M5) — Optimising. Required for the BPT Loop to run smoothly.

    The levels themselves, the five elements they are scored against, and the scoring rule are in DBJ CMM.

    The organisation KPI#

    The DBJ CMM level is the essential KPI of the organisation. Measured before and after, it is how an organisation measures itself: not by what it delivered, but by what it is now able to deliver. Taking that measurement is the Maturity Assessment.

    Basically this is where we train premiership league teams. Into qualified navigators of the AI enabled business.
